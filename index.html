Manufacturing Process

From waste to rich compost

Vermicompost is made with the help of selected earthworms in five simple steps.

  1. 1

    Raw Material Selection

    Suitable organic raw material is selected.

  2. 2

    Material Preparation

    The material is prepared for composting.

  3. 3

    Earthworm Decomposition

    Earthworms help break the material down.

  4. 4

    Natural Maturation

    The compost matures naturally.

  5. 5

    Collection & Packaging

    Finished vermicompost is collected and packed.
